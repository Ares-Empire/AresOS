## Lead's triage

- **1, fix, with a simpler design.** Stopping the worker inside a running backend leaves races (a starting worker, a teardown whose slot is cleared before labwc confirms). Instead: when sharing goes Off, or the last session ends, the backend runs its existing teardown (labwc's confirmation within 1 s, else exit as today) and then **exits the process**; sockets close with it, and the frontend activates a fresh backend for the next request. A worker that starts re-reads the switch after connecting and exits the process if it reads Off. `--release` during that teardown waits for its outcome or falls back to its pidfd kill as today. The VM gate fails on `pidfd_getfd` errors except a descriptor that vanished.
- **2, check fixed, the rest a known limit.** The image check must require `rpm -V deskflow` to exit 0. A later manual `dnf reinstall` or `rpm-ostree override replace` of Deskflow by the owner could bring back Fedora's entry; image updates repeat the exclusion. Known limit, not a rebuilt RPM.
- **3, fix.** Rejection is the default answer of the pairing prompt (Enter saves nothing). Verify from an empty profile with a real Deskflow client peer and the real dialog where the harness allows: Enter and rejection save nothing, deliberate acceptance saves exactly one fingerprint, a second certificate is refused.

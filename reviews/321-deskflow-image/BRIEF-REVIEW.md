6. **Off can leave an active capture forwarding.** `briefs/321-deskflow-image.md:25,54,69` relies on a second process calling `--revoke` after the durable switch changes. 7. **The release timeout measures a D-Bus reply, not the release of the grab.** `briefs/321-deskflow-image.md:26,31,50` covers a process-wide `SIGSTOP`. `ForceRelease` can still reply while Wayland teardown is only queued (`os/input-portal/src/portal.rs:1139–1175,1908–1915`). 8. **The passwordless root action is underspecified.** `briefs/321-deskflow-image.md:24,54` gives one executable two authorization levels and points to an audit that checks decisions by action ID but does not parse `pkexec` executable or argument bindings (`os/ci/polkit-check.js:83–96,198–203`).

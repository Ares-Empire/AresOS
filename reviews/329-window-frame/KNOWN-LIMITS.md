- At 1920 × 1080 and scale 1, the two Jersey 10 title crops contained only the exact ink and bar colours. The long title ends in an ellipsis. Other output scales and rendering backends remain unmeasured. [Title crops](evidence/sheet-01.png)
- In labwc 0.9.6, Alt+Tab sends the world `KBD_LEAVE`. After Escape the world is still active and receives the same relative pointer delta with its lock intact, but it receives neither press nor release of an unbound `A`. Switching away releases the lock. While the pointer is over the uncovered world, it receives relative and absolute motion. Switching back sends `KBD_ENTER`, relocks the pointer and restores key delivery. The same sequence occurs with main's baseline `rc.xml`. [Switcher event comparison](evidence/06-switcher.json)
- labwc places each switcher label below the thumbnail inside its tile and centres it. The minimized test client keeps its thumbnail, and clicking a tile chooses it immediately. The synthetic apps have no installed app icons in the harness, so the captures show their names and client pixels. [Switcher sheet](evidence/sheet-06.png)

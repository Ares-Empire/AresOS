# Design re-check 30: draft 27 (decision 25, Chrome as the light tool)

Reviewer: Codex `gpt-6-astra`, fresh context, 2026-11-08. Reviewed draft 27 at `0ed244e6`.

Blocking findings:

- [Resuming submissions](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/design/CONTROL.md:491) is not yet equivalent to continuing the stopped step. Native `requestSubmit()` runs validation and submit handlers; native `submit()` bypasses both. Converting every intercepted `submit()` into `requestSubmit()` can therefore introduce callbacks, cancel a previously valid submission, or repeatedly intercept an onsubmit handler’s `submit()`. Saving the native function and binding a one-use mark do not fix that difference. Preserve the intercepted submission kind and test both paths. The same-form/same-submitter requirement otherwise addresses replacement before resumption. [HTML specification](https://html.spec.whatwg.org/multipage/forms.html#dom-form-requestsubmit-dev).

- [Compose binding](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/design/CONTROL.md:507) still has a hole: “since its last approved action.” Fill a compose area outside a form, approve an upload or unrelated action, then prepare Send. The compose text now falls outside the recorded state and can change while approval waits. Existing drafts Claude never filled are also excluded. Bind the current compose content relevant to the send, independently of unrelated approvals; refuse when that cannot be established.

The other former blocking points are closed at design level. Newly encountered confirmations receive their own classification; adapter exemptions require an exact match. Unknown destinations are disclosed honestly. Approval-page exclusions cover the named routes. Page-authored `javascript:` controls remain classified activations, while tool-supplied script navigation is refused. Submissions after the interception limit are explicitly accepted residual activity under decision 25.

Should-fix:

- [Password extraction](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/design/CONTROL.md:374) remains incomplete. Returning “unknown” after receiving plaintext is filtering after collection. Chromium’s accessibility value path can return unmasked text, including when text-security masking is absent. Require exclusion before extraction and inspect browser-to-extension responses in brief 20; the listed extension/host/service checks miss that boundary. [Chromium source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/chromium/chromium/main/third_party/blink/renderer/modules/accessibility/ax_node_object.cc).

- Test 4 says every other confirmation asks, whereas the rule and FEATURES require a listed word. Align that qualification and add the counterexamples above.

The download, outcome, service/extension and section 2/10 corrections are sound. Repeated qualification, cancellation, crash and adapter-update tests are restored. PLAN and STATE preserve decisions 24–25, but the remaining defects prevent readiness.

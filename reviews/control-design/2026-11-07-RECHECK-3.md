Draft 4 introduces these issues:

- **Blocking, within #4 — Human input can release Claude’s queued work.** The [new exemption](../../docs/design/CONTROL.md:327) permits requests caused by the owner’s input, including in other guarded tabs. **Scenario:** Claude queues deletion of item 42 without typing text; the owner later clicks an unrelated “Refresh list” control in another tab, whose handler flushes the shared queue through POST. The request passes despite no approval of that deletion. **Fix:** exempt only operations proven independent of unresolved Claude effects; otherwise hold or refuse. Also reconcile this exemption with the existing rule that human sends inside the Claude group remain held. Counted once under #4.

- **Should-fix — Restore invalidation also appears to prohibit historical status and repair.** [“Every id of an earlier generation is refused”](../../docs/design/CONTROL.md:121) does not distinguish submission from inspection or repair. **Scenario:** after restoring, a delivered mail needs its missing Sent copy repaired, but its operation ID is now rejected. **Fix:** invalidate old submissions and approvals while retaining historical status/reconciliation; permit evidence-backed, non-resubmitting repairs under fresh operation IDs.

# Design re-check 32: draft 29 (decision 25, Chrome as the light tool)

Reviewer: Codex `gpt-6-astra`, fresh context, 2026-11-08. Reviewed draft 29 at `d3a8f26e`.

Two blocking findings remain:

- The [outermost-item rule](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/design/CONTROL.md:563) still permits incomplete binding. Put the message body in a plain editable div, beside a named region containing recipients and Send, with no enclosing dialog or form. That region is the sole candidate and contains content; the “same dialog” refusal never applies. The body can change throughout approval undetected. A sibling read-only preview has the same problem. Broaden the binding or refuse this layout, and add both cases to test 3.

- [Resuming the saved event object](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/design/CONTROL.md:536) introduces a mutable-input gap. After interception returns, the page can call `event.initEvent("dblclick", …)` while approval waits. The DOM can remain unchanged, yet approval now dispatches a different event and invokes different handlers. Saving native `dispatchEvent()` does not freeze its argument; this mutation is explicitly supported by the [DOM specification](https://dom.spec.whatwg.org/#dom-event-initevent). Require rejection of changed or reused events, or refuse resumption when preservation cannot be established. Extend test 2.

The original non-cancelable-click problem is addressed for wrapped calls: interception precedes delivery. The specified capture-listener fallback also avoids replay correctly: cancellable events stay stopped without resumption; non-cancellable events become `unknown` without resumption.

Previous should-fix items are closed at design level: consumed marks survive re-entry, password checks precede every read, and qualification includes late changes, shadow roots and cross-origin frames. One contradiction remains: [PLAN](/Users/pab/AresOS-design-chrome/docs/control/PLAN.md:617) says every wrapper bypass becomes `unknown`, whereas section 6 distinguishes cancellable events. Align that summary.

These are design gaps beyond the remaining implementation qualification.

## New findings

1. **BLOCKER — The outer request can start browser effects after inheriting a barrier.** During the first `stage_file` of an approved upload, the owner turns read-only on for another site. The nested `chrome_sites` write hits an undone first fence, replies with `storage_limit` and hands the barrier back. `Bridge.call` drains and returns its successful staging reply. The upload then sends another `stage_file` and `input` with no intervening guard. That input installs the file and fires page events. The probe also recorded `maintenance` and `tabs` after the same failure arrived during `configure`. Require a barrier check before each new browser command and before post-`configure` housekeeping, while still draining the outstanding reply. Add a mutation that omits the mid-dispatch check. Injecting the fault during the final `tabs` call, as i7.2 does, leaves no follow-up dispatch to catch.

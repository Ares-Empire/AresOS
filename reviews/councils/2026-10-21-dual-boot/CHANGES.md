## Simpler-route evaluation

[OS-BASE §8](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#8-install-targets-image-delivery-and-choosing-an-os) now explicitly acknowledges **current automatic alongside-Windows installation**, its shared ESP and the manual steps it removes. Automatic simplifies installation, but makes undo require file ownership/hash reconciliation on Windows storage. A qualified per-file shared-ESP write manifest is not available; possible Fedora/vendor and fallback paths are identified from sources, without pretending they are the exact installed payload. Future shared-ESP qualification must reject **every `\EFI\Microsoft` modification** and supply precise addition/overwrite reversal. **Recommend the separate-ESP legacy route** under the brief's safest-reversible default.

Maintainer registry/backend/UID research is removed from the owner's action sequence and retained in the design/release gate. The owner has one route, one media writer and one record sheet.

## Implementation evidence

The [read-only helper](../../../docs/install-tools/InstallRecord.psm1) avoids asking a novice to interpret Windows NT volume names or convert UEFI binary structures. Importing it performs no hardware queries. Only the owner calls its inventory command; the build run tests synthetic data and compiles the native reader without calling it. It imports no disk-write or firmware-write API. See the [05b report](../../05b-dual-boot-fixes/REPORT.md) for exact commands/results and remaining limits.

New sources are dated **2026-10-21** at the relevant guide/design passages and [record source list](../../../docs/INSTALL-RECORD.md#sources-and-limits--checked-2026-10-21). The original verdict/member files and brief remain unchanged.

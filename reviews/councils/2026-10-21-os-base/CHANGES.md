# OS base council: revision map

2026-10-21 · [brief 02b](../../../briefs/02b-os-base-revision.md) · [verdict](VERDICT.md) · [revised design](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md) · PR #2

All seven blocking findings are addressed in the design. “Resolved” below means a specified contract and acceptance gate, **not implemented or hardware-tested behavior**. Sources for external facts are linked and dated in the design's Appendix F. The chair can follow these links directly; the verdict and member reviews remain unchanged.

| # | Finding | Resolution or explicit deferral | Check / gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — blocking | Backup credentials die with the SSD | [B6](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b6-backups-that-survive-losing-the-drive) requires independent repository location, password, backend/second-factor recovery, instructions and matching game manifest. [A2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#a2-complete-recovery-and-private-machine-state) separates private machine-state recovery. | P2: original SSD unavailable; restore known builds, inventory/UUID, marker and app IDs on a fresh system before valuable saves. |
| 2 — blocking | Lid/key/critical battery bypass clean save | [§7](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#7-everyday-controls-and-safe-power), [B3](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b3-power-events-app-checkpoint-and-helper-failure): static no-sleep/no-automatic-power defaults, system coordinator and guardian independent of app helper; direct save channel, app checkpoint, early bounded battery reserve and no remote-backup wait. Hard power loss is explicitly outside an unconditional save guarantee. | P2 before hardware: loading, >5-second save and maintenance; kill helper and separately coordinator; inject events, verify no bypass and reopened saved edits. Selected UPower configuration must be proven, or image fails. |
| 3 — blocking | Unsaved ordinary app work lost | [B3](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b3-power-events-app-checkpoint-and-helper-failure) requires cancellable “close your apps first,” including gameplay and explicit background-work confirmation, before desktop stop/native shutdown. Missing helper/window enumeration is not an empty app list. | P2: unsaved editor/browser form; cancel preserves both and usable desktop; complete only after safe manual closure. Critical battery gets an explicitly limited emergency policy. |
| 4 — blocking | Incomplete previous system/recovery and lost machine settings | [§9](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#9-updates-backups-and-recovery), [A2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#a2-complete-recovery-and-private-machine-state), [C](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#appendix-c--installer-safety-specification): complete OS/kernel/NVIDIA set, independent bootable offline kit, compatible game/state checkpoint and selective restore/reprovision table, including Wi-Fi and recovery login. | Before **any** update: offline previous-deployment boot plus separate broken-root and damaged-ESP recovery; NVIDIA, network, login, world, protected disk and BootOrder checks. Automation may wait until P4; recovery readiness cannot. |
| 5 — blocking | Competing update authorities and non-convergent upgrades | [A1](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#a1-image-and-sole-maintenance-authority): AresOS owns managed mutations, masks uupd timer/automatic/manual services, disables other native/autostart paths, inventories exact image and reconciles removals/config/services. Weekly/urgent security review starts with P1; controlled manual maintenance precedes UI automation. | First-image exclusions; P4 fresh B versus A→B with removed package/renamed service/config migration; fire native timers and entry points outside maintenance, asserting no installed or staged changes. |
| 6 — blocking | No concrete atomic NVIDIA comparison | [§1](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#1-base-and-os-as-code) compares Bazzite desktop NVIDIA with Ubuntu for labwc replacement, boot/image trust, update ownership and recovery effort; [§3](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#3-secure-boot-and-windows-return) applies accepted installation MOK without promising zero BitLocker prompts. | Bazzite selected; P1 resolves real digest/backend, P3 qualifies signed NVIDIA, isolation/F12 and Windows return; Ubuntu fallback on qualification failure. Offline recovery remains mandatory. |
| 7 — blocking | Mod commands conflict with OS-owned launches | [B1](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b1-mod-and-os-interface-v1-and-package-01-migration): versioned `aresos-shell/1.0`, list/open/focus/lifecycle messages, peer/nonce/request identity, incompatibility rejection and Windows-local adapter. Schema-2 migration preserves all IDs/appearance/pins and backs up old commands; Linux never executes legacy argv. | P2 actual migrated `notepad` item→helper→editor, old saved IDs, failed/incompatible helper and duplicate request tests. Separate guardian lifecycle channel survives app-helper death. |
| 8 — non-blocking | Memory pressure disables recovery | [§2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#2-graphics-memory-and-power), [B5](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b5-memory-gpu-and-restart-pressure): initial heap/whole-process budgets, separated recovery/game/app groups, explicit zram/AresOS-only overflow and no pressure restart loop. | Baseline P2; representative study/gameplay workload and measured RAM/VRAM in P5. Simultaneous games deferred until headroom, responsiveness and comfort gates pass. |
| 9 — non-blocking | Slow endless crash loops | [B2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b2-supervision-and-focus): three consecutive failures in addition to the short-window limit, persistent recovery latch; reset only after 30 healthy minutes or explicit Retry. | P2: ready-then-crash every four minutes including supervisor restart; finite recovery. |
| 10 — non-blocking | Background closure steals typing focus | [§4](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#4-session-apps-and-recovery), [B2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b2-supervision-and-focus): foreground final-window return only, preserve background focus, clear held inputs and handle dialogs. | P2 continuous browser typing while background terminal closes; foreground/dialog/Alt+Tab/pointer cases separately. |
| 11 — non-blocking | Hardware evidence comes too late | [E](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#appendix-e--phase-2-packages-in-build-order) replaces P1–P12 with minimal image/safe installer → integrated world/app/save/tested backup → early P3 hardware trial, including the missing Nether mod dependency. | F12→NVIDIA Nether→app open/close→clean shutdown→Windows with USB present/absent, plus altered-world restore, before optional base-specific work. |
| 12 — non-blocking | Sleep has no matching resume contract | [§7](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#7-everyday-controls-and-safe-power), [D](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#appendix-d--acceptance-trials-not-executed-results), [E](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#appendix-e--phase-2-packages-in-build-order): suspend stays disabled system-wide. Retained-process versus stop/relaunch decision is explicitly deferred to P7. | Before enabling: specify resume, player position, foreground focus and wake latency; test away from hub/foreground browser, no duplicates, twenty hardware cycles. Lid save/stop is explicitly not suspend. |
| 13 — non-blocking | FPS caps do not establish daily comfort | [§2](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#2-graphics-memory-and-power) specifies active/app-focused/idle rendering, simulation/audio and blanking; P5 must establish owner-accepted measured limits. | Same browser workload stopped/hidden/visible on AC/battery, idle and charger transitions; power/temperature/noise/return latency. Failure retains save/stop fallback; no unmeasured comfort claim. |

The owner decisions also add [B4's normal-gameplay lifecycle](../../../docs/design/OS-BASE.md#b4-normal-gameplay-and-return): a separate instance without desktop rules, desktop save/stop before playing on 16 GB, actual-game focus/exit tracking, and one return to the hub. Lunar and two resident games are explicit later qualification gates. The opening ten-line summary explains the experience and remaining uncertainty; implementation details stay in appendices.
